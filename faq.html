Everything you need to know about WaveList, playlists, and audio playback

WaveList is a modern, responsive web application inspired by Spotify and music streaming hubs. It allows music enthusiasts to explore popular artists, curated albums, manage personalized song playlists with full CRUD functionality, and search millions of live songs via our public Music API.

Yes! All song additions, edits, and deletions performed on the Songs page are automatically synchronized and persisted in your browser's localStorage. Even when you refresh your browser or close the tab, your playlist changes remain preserved.

The Music API page communicates directly with the public iTunes Search API using modern JavaScript fetch() requests. It retrieves live JSON data, handles errors gracefully, and provides direct 30-second playable HTML5 audio streams with album artwork and genre classifications.

Both authentication and your chosen theme (Dark/Light) are managed client-side using localStorage. When you sign in or switch themes, your preferences are saved across all pages of the site and remain active across sessions until you sign out or clear your browser data.
